Privacy notice

Effective October 2, 2026.

Application and account data

The local studio stores creative work, source and rights evidence, review decisions, schedules, connected-account identity, granted scopes, authorization deadlines, and delivery outcomes. Credentials are protected for the signed-in Windows user and excluded from portable backups and reviewer exports. Creative files and ordinary database fields are not individually encrypted. The workspace may also be synchronized by the owner’s OneDrive configuration.

YouTube API Services

After the owner authorizes the connection, the application uses YouTube API Services to identify the selected channel. For an expressly approved release, it may send the reviewed video, thumbnail, captions and metadata, and read processing, ownership, visibility and schedule state. The application keeps local video identifiers and delivery evidence to reconcile interruptions and avoid duplicate uploads. It does not sell YouTube API data, use it for advertising, or use it to train story-generation models.

The current YouTube authorization uses the youtube.force-ssl scope because the implemented workflow includes caption upload and video-status changes. Google’s grant has broader platform authority than the application’s local controls. The application does not implement automatic deletion of public videos or comment management.

Meta and TikTok

For Facebook and Instagram, the application stores the selected Page and linked professional-profile identity, approved permissions, access deadlines and exact delivery outcomes. For TikTok, Login Kit uses user.info.basic to verify the authorized profile; Direct Post uses video.publish and video.upload only for an exact owner-approved video and settings. Login alone does not upload content.

Storage, sharing and retention

The core application runs on the owner’s computer and contains no advertising or third-party tracking scripts. Data is sent to a platform only for an authorized connection, identity check, backup operation or release. Account-access data and delivery receipts are retained for connection health, duplicate prevention, audit and recovery until the applicable reviewed removal action. Creative work and owner decisions are preserved separately from removable provider credentials and API data.

Your controls

Access can be revoked in Google’s third-party connection settings or the corresponding connected-app controls at Meta and TikTok. Revoking access does not delete public posts or cancel every remote schedule. Story Studio’s Account data & privacy area provides a local inventory and reviewed managed-data removal. See Data & access for the process and its limits.

Use of YouTube API Services is also governed by the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
