Chapter & Lantern Story Studio

Chapter & Lantern Story Studio is an owner-operated local application for preparing, reviewing and publishing illustrated story videos.

The application uses YouTube API Services and authorized Meta and TikTok connections only for the owner’s selected accounts. Connecting an account does not approve a post. A finished release, its destination, visibility and schedule are reviewed separately before delivery.
