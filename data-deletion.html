Account data and access

To request support or removal of supported account data, email chapterandlantern.studio@gmail.com from the address associated with the owner account and identify the affected platform. Do not include a password, verification code, client secret, or access token.

  1. Revoke the application’s access in the platform’s connected-app or security settings when immediate provider-side revocation is needed.
  2. In Story Studio, open Account data & privacy and inspect the affected platform inventory.
  3. Resolve scheduled, in-progress, or uncertain deliveries on the platform. Revoking access does not cancel remote schedules or delete public posts.
  4. Review and approve the exact local managed-data removal operation. Creative work is preserved unless it is separately selected and reviewed.
  5. Review backup, synchronized, and platform-held copies separately. Local deletion cannot certify removal from OneDrive version history, another device, or a platform.

Requests are reviewed against the local records and normally acknowledged within 30 days. Platform-held content must also be managed through that platform where required.
